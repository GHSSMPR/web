FROM THE DESK OF


Principal

Dear Teachers, Non-Teaching Staff, Students, Parents and Well-Wishers, It gives me immense pleasure and a sense of responsibility to join Government Higher Secondary School, Mahadevpur, as the Principal. I feel honoured to be a part of this esteemed institution and look forward to working together with all stakeholders for its continued growth and development. I firmly believe that a school is not merely a place of academic learning but an institution where charater is shaped, values are nurtured, talents are discovered and future citizens are prepared. Our collective responsibility is to create an environment that promotes academic excellence, discipline, punctuality, mutual respect, creativity and all-round development of students. As we move forward , my priorities will be to strengthen the academic environment, improve student's learning outcomes, maintain discipline and regularity, encourage co-curricular and extracurricular activities, and ensure a safe, inclusive and student friendly atmosphere within the school. I sincerely seek the cooperation and active participation of our dedicated teachers, non-teaching staff, students, parents, School Management Committee and the local community. I believe that teamwork, transparency, mutual understanding and a shared sense of responsibility are the keys to acieving our common goals. Let us work together with commitment, sincerity and a positive spirit to take our institution to grater heights and make it a centre of excellence. Together, let us strive for excellence, inspire young minds and build a brighter future. With best wishes, Dipty Tayeng, Principal Govt. Higher Secondary School,Mahadevpur District-Namsai (Arunachal Pradesh)
